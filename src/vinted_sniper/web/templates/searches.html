What the statuses mean
{{ ui.status("watching", "Running") }}

Checked recently and working. New listings arrive as soon as Vinted shows them in search.

{{ ui.status("quiet", "Quiet") }}

Running fine, but nothing new for a while. That's normal for narrow searches and at night.

{{ ui.status("stale", "Same results lately") }}

Vinted answers, but keeps showing this search the same listings. Nothing you need to do; it usually sorts itself out within the hour.

{{ ui.status("stuck", "Waiting on Vinted") }}

Vinted answered strangely, or we couldn't reach it. We keep trying on our own.

{{ ui.status("blocked", "Slowed down by Vinted") }}

Vinted asked us to slow down. We wait a few minutes and try again. Your Vinted account is not affected: we never use one.

{{ ui.status("paused", "Paused") }}

You paused it. No checks and no alerts until you resume.
