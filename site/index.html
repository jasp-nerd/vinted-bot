Install

Vinted doesn't tell you when your search gets something new. This does.

vinted-sniper watches your Vinted searches and sends each new listing to Discord, Telegram or your phone, with the price you'd actually pay. It's free, open source, and runs on your own computer.

Install for free See it on GitHub

Windows, Mac, Linux, Raspberry Pi, Docker or a NAS.

  • No Vinted login
  • Nothing to pay, ever
  • No tracking

It tells you when it stops working

Most alert tools fail silently. You find out a week later, when you notice it's been quiet. vinted-sniper checks its own work and tells you, per search and in plain words, when something's wrong and what it's doing about it.

A quiet search at night is normal. A search showing the same listings for three hours isn't, and you'll hear about it.

Everything you need, nothing that needs your login

It does everything except buy or message for you. Those need your Vinted account, and that's where account bans come from.

  • The price you'd actually pay

    Set a maximum of €35 and it means €35 with buyer protection included. Vinted's own price filter ignores the fee.

  • Tells you when it stops working

    Each search shows whether it's watching, quiet, stuck or blocked, and you get a message when one needs a look.

  • No account, nothing to ban

    It reads public listings anonymously and never asks for your Vinted login. There's no account for Vinted to restrict.

  • Deal badges

    It learns the typical price for each search and flags listings that are clearly under it. Send only those if you like.

  • Filters Vinted doesn't have

    Words a title must include, with "this or that". Words to skip, like "for parts" or "box only". Regular expressions, if that's your thing.

  • Seller filters and a blocklist

    Minimum rating and reviews, which countries sellers ship from, and a block button for the seller who tags every listing with your search.

  • Quiet hours

    Overnight alerts wait for the morning, arrive without a sound, or get skipped. Your choice, per search.

  • Price drops and sold

    Watch a listing and hear when its price drops or it sells. Watch a seller and hear when they upload something new.

  • Every Vinted country

    All 27 country sites, from vinted.fr to vinted.co.uk. One search can cover several countries at once.

  • Alerts where you already are

    Discord, Telegram, ntfy on your phone, email, and 100 more through Apprise. Each search picks its own places.

  • A dashboard with a live feed

    Every find in a photo grid that updates as listings arrive, with a sound if you want one. Save, hide or block from there.

  • "Why wasn't this sent?"

    Every listing a search skipped is kept for a while, with the reason: over your price, a skipped word, a low-rated seller.

Install it in a few minutes

One command installs it, starts it and opens the dashboard. No admin rights, no Docker, nothing outside your own user folder. Run the same command again to update.

  1. Open PowerShell: click Start, type PowerShell and press Enter.

  2. Paste this and press Enter:

    powershell -ExecutionPolicy ByPass -c "irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jasp-nerd/vinted-sniper/main/install.ps1 | iex"

  3. It asks whether to start by itself when you sign in. Say yes, unless you'd rather start it yourself.

  4. Your browser opens the dashboard at http://localhost:8000. The app runs in the background, with no window to keep open.

Keep the PC awake while it's plugged in: Settings, System, Power, and set sleep to Never.

Update or remove it

To update, run the same command again. To remove it (your searches are kept):

powershell -ExecutionPolicy ByPass -c '$env:VINTED_SNIPER_UNINSTALL=1; irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jasp-nerd/vinted-sniper/main/install.ps1 | iex'

  1. Open Terminal: press Cmd+Space, type Terminal and press Enter.

  2. Paste this and press Enter:

    curl -LsSf https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jasp-nerd/vinted-sniper/main/install.sh | sh

  3. It asks whether to keep your Mac awake while it's plugged in, and whether to start at login. Yes to both gives you reliable alerts. macOS then shows "Background Items Added". That's expected.

  4. Your browser opens the dashboard at http://localhost:8000.

Update or remove it

To update, run the same command again. To remove it (your searches are kept):

curl -LsSf https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jasp-nerd/vinted-sniper/main/install.sh | sh -s -- --uninstall

  1. In a terminal, run this. On a Raspberry Pi, use 64-bit Raspberry Pi OS.

    curl -LsSf https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jasp-nerd/vinted-sniper/main/install.sh | sh

  2. Say yes to starting at login. To keep it running with nobody logged in (handy on a Pi), the installer may ask you to run:

    sudo loginctl enable-linger $USER

  3. Open http://localhost:8000. A Pi without a screen? Run this on your own computer, then open the same address there (use your Pi's user and name):

    ssh -L 8000:localhost:8000 pi@raspberrypi.local

Questions people ask

Something else? Ask on GitHub.

Will Vinted ban me?

Your account, no: there isn't one involved. vinted-sniper reads public listings anonymously. It never logs in and never asks for your Vinted details, so there's no account for Vinted to restrict.

What Vinted can do is stop answering your internet connection (your IP address) for a while if it sees too many requests. That's temporary. The defaults are one check a minute per search, and vinted-sniper slows down by itself when Vinted asks it to.

Is it really free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT licence, with no paid tier, no account and no ads. It runs on your own computer, so there's no server for anyone to pay for.

If you'd rather not keep a computer on, a small rented server costs a few euros a month. That goes to the host, not to this project.

Do I need to leave my computer on?

Yes. Alerts only arrive while the computer running it is on, awake and online. A laptop that sleeps sends nothing while asleep. The Mac installer can keep a plugged-in Mac awake for you.

For alerts around the clock, run it on a Raspberry Pi, a NAS or an old laptop left plugged in. Your phone just receives the alerts.

Why isn't it instant?

Vinted shows new listings in its search a few minutes after they're posted, so every tool, paid or free, sees them a little late. vinted-sniper checks every minute by default and usually alerts you within a few minutes of a listing going up.

That's fast enough for collectors, size hunters and most bargains. If you're racing autobuy bots for underpriced consoles, no alert will win that, and we'd rather say so.

Is it legal? Is it allowed?

Vinted's terms prohibit automated access. That's a contract between you and Vinted, not a law: breaking it isn't a crime, but it gives Vinted grounds to stop serving you. In the EU, reading publicly available pages isn't in itself unlawful.

This tool reads public listings anonymously, at an unhurried pace, and never buys, lists or messages for you. Running it is your call. If Vinted asks you to stop, stop. More in the notes on rules and risks. Not legal advice.

Can it buy things or message sellers for me?

No, and it won't. Buying needs a logged-in Vinted account, and that's where account restrictions come from. Each alert has a Buy button; you tap it yourself.

What happens to my data?

It stays in a file on your computer. vinted-sniper stores only what an alert needs and deletes listings after 30 days. It sends nothing anywhere except the alerts you set up, plus a daily check for new versions that you can turn off. This website has no trackers or cookies either.

Which countries does it work in?

All 27 Vinted country sites, including vinted.fr, .de, .nl, .be, .es, .it, .pl and .co.uk. Prices are shown in the site's own currency, and alerts use the site's language for their few fixed words.
